# Manuscript source and template

`main.tex` is the canonical paper. Named and anonymous exports share four
main-content pages plus references; author identification is omitted from the
anonymous export. `supplement-main.tex` produces a separate supplementary PDF
from `extended-methods.tex` and `demo-appendix.tex`. See
[the manuscript build](../docs/PAPER.md) and
[reproduction](../docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

`IEEEtran.cls` is copied unchanged from the
[official RSS LaTeX template](https://roboticsconference.org/docs/paper-template-latex.tar.gz).
Its original notices and LaTeX Project Public License 1.3 terms are retained.
`template-manifest.json` records the archive and class hashes.

`references.bib` supplies the `plainnat` bibliography without external URLs.
The class license is separate from the research code's Apache-2.0 license and
the manuscript's CC BY 4.0 license.
