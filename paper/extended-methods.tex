\section{Additional Evaluation Details}
This supplement accompanies \emph{Beyond Dodge Rate: Testing Humanoid Dodgeball
Under Camera Failures}. The main paper reports the primary recovery test,
camera-input comparison, shared-throw effects, and their uncertainty. The details
below describe the exploratory control and recorded-data checks.

\subsection{Shared ball trajectories}
For trajectory index $i=0,\ldots,63$, donor seed $9000+i$ supplies a healthy-condition
throw from $\pi_1$ when $i$ is even and $\pi_2$ when $i$ is odd. This selects 32
trajectories per donor policy without filtering outcomes. Simulation seeds
10000--10063 replay the pool with both policies and all five camera conditions.
These are 640 exploratory trials, not newly sampled physical trajectories or
independent training runs.

At launch, the evaluator retains native collision settings, accounting, and
random draws, then replaces only the ball's initial position and velocity before
integration. Exact throw parameters match across all ten policy-condition
combinations per seed. Robot states can differ between policies. The target
label describes the donor's original aim, not the receiving robot's body part.
Twenty preflight trials use two separate simulation seeds. Two additional
healthy-condition replays of the first control seed reproduce complete trial
records and verify live ball position and velocity before integration.

\subsection{Trajectory and uncertainty checks}
Hits resolve at native contact; misses resolve when the simulator ends the throw.
Resolution is not a closest-approach measurement. Area exits use 50 Hz samples
without interpolation; a tie with resolution counts as a by-resolution exit.
The 1 m score fails at the first sampled exit even if the robot later returns.
For stale-input restoration pairs, launch states, throws, camera schedules, and
all physical samples before restoration match exactly. Every stale-input throw
resolves by two seconds within numerical tolerance.

Paired intervals resample complete evaluation seeds with all their conditions.
The shared-throw input-effect difference retains both policies in each resampled
seed. Bootstrap runs use 10,000 resamples and seed 20261014. Intervals are nominal
per comparison, not multiplicity-adjusted. The trial archives retain per-condition
Wilson intervals, camera schedules, physical traces, outcome flags, and matching
checks. The original 2,496-trial package includes exploratory cohorts as well as
the fixed 192-seed input test. Recovery uses a separate 160-trial pilot and a
640-trial fixed cohort. Exploratory controls and post-hoc scoring analyses are
not pooled into that fixed estimate.
